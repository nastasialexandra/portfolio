MBA Thesis · Product Discovery / Strategy

Thesis — Desk Sensors as a New Product Opportunity

Process

Researched market fit through literature review, scenario planning, and 11 semi-structured customer interviews, translating insights into product and adoption hypotheses.

Key findings
  • Unpredictable office attendance and real-estate costs were the strongest demand drivers.
  • Desk sensors sit between early adopters and early majority — the value is there, but adoption depends heavily on company size and hybrid-work culture.
  • Privacy concerns (especially in the Nordics & Germany) and subscription fatigue emerged as major barriers.
  • Strongest opportunity: integrating desk sensing into Framery Connect, rather than treating it as a standalone product.
